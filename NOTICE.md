## Public research status

Adaptive Invariant Computation is an early-stage research programme. The public
documents describe hypotheses, conceptual foundations, and proposed research
directions. They should not be interpreted as evidence that the proposed
mechanisms have already been experimentally validated.

## Background research

The programme draws conceptually from work including:

- Bindu Recursive Compression Theory (BRCT);
- invariant-space and geometric representation research;
- inversion and reversion as tests of information preservation;
- recursive representation reduction and efficient AI.

References to these areas establish research lineage and do not grant rights to
unpublished implementations derived from them.
